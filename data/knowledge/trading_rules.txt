SOL/USDC Trading Rules and Risk Management

Position Sizing:
- Maximum position size is 5% of portfolio (500 basis points)
- Scale position size with confidence: below 60% confidence use 2%, above 75% use 4%
- Never exceed max position regardless of confidence

Risk Limits:
- Maximum daily loss: 3% of portfolio (300 basis points)
- Maximum drawdown from peak: 10% (1000 basis points)
- Circuit breaker: halt trading if daily loss exceeds 2.5%

Entry Conditions:
- Minimum confidence threshold: 55%
- RSI must not be in extreme territory (below 20 or above 80) for new positions
- MACD should confirm the direction of the prediction
- Volume should be at least 0.8x average for signal validity

Exit Rules:
- Take profit at 3-4% gain
- Stop loss at 2% loss
- Trailing stop: move stop to breakeven after 1.5% profit
- Time-based exit: close positions open longer than 4 hours
