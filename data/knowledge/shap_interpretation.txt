SHAP Feature Interpretation Guide

How to interpret SHAP values for SOL/USDC trading:

RSI (Relative Strength Index):
- Positive SHAP = RSI pushes prediction toward UP (often means oversold conditions)
- Negative SHAP = RSI pushes toward DOWN (often means overbought)
- Large magnitude = RSI is a dominant signal in this prediction

MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence):
- Positive SHAP = bullish crossover or histogram expansion
- Negative SHAP = bearish crossover or histogram contraction
- MACD signal line crossovers are the strongest signals

Bollinger Position:
- Positive SHAP = price near lower band (potential bounce)
- Negative SHAP = price near upper band (potential reversal)
- Position outside bands indicates extreme conditions

Volume Spike:
- Positive SHAP = elevated volume supporting the move
- Negative SHAP = low volume suggesting weak conviction
- Volume > 1.5x average with matching direction is strong confirmation

Momentum:
- Positive SHAP = accelerating price in predicted direction
- Negative SHAP = decelerating or contrary momentum
- Momentum divergences from price are important warnings

Volatility:
- Positive SHAP = volatility favoring the predicted direction
- Negative SHAP = volatility suggesting caution
- High volatility with low confidence = reduce position size
